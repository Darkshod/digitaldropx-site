How to Price Your Digital Products (Pricing Guide)

September 1, 2026 • 12 min read

Pricing digital products is both an art and a science. Price too low and you leave money on the table. Price too high and you scare away buyers. Here's how to find the sweet spot.

The 3 Pricing Strategies

1. Cost-Plus Pricing

Calculate your costs, add your desired profit margin. Simple but often underprices value.

  • Calculate time spent creating
  • Add tools/software costs
  • Add your hourly rate × hours
  • Add 20-50% profit margin

2. Value-Based Pricing

Price based on the value to the customer. Best for high-impact products.

  • How much time will this save?
  • How much money will this help them make?
  • What's the alternative cost?
  • What's the perceived value?

3. Competition-Based Pricing

Look at what competitors charge. Price similar or differentiate.

  • Research 5-10 competitors
  • Note their prices and features
  • Position yourself in the middle
  • Differentiate on value, not price

Pricing Psychology Tips

  • Charm pricing: $29 instead of $30 (feels cheaper)
  • Anchoring: Show original price crossed out
  • Bundles: "Get 3 for $49 (save $38)"
  • Tiered pricing: Offer 3 options (good, better, best)
  • Scarcity: "Only 50 licenses at this price"
