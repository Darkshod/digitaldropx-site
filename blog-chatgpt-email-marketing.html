How to Use ChatGPT for Email Marketing in 2026

September 1, 2026 • 10 min read

ChatGPT has become an essential tool for email marketers. From writing subject lines to creating complete email sequences, AI can save you hours while improving results.

1. Subject Line Generator

Subject lines determine whether your email gets opened. Here's a prompt that generates high-converting subject lines:

Subject Line Prompt
Generate 10 email subject lines for [TOPIC]. Requirements: - Under 50 characters each - Use numbers, questions, or urgency - Avoid spam words - Include 3 curiosity-based, 3 benefit-based, 3 urgency-based, 1 personalized Target audience: [AUDIENCE]

2. Welcome Email Sequence

A good welcome sequence builds trust and drives first purchases. Here's the prompt:

Welcome Sequence Prompt
Write a 5-email welcome sequence for new subscribers. Business: [DESCRIPTION] Goal: [GOAL] For each email provide: - Subject line + 2 alternatives - Preview text - Body (150 words) - CTA button text Email 1: Welcome + freebie delivery Email 2: Your story/mission Email 3: Quick win tip Email 4: Social proof Email 5: Soft sell

3. Sales Email Template

When you have a product to promote, this prompt creates compelling sales emails:

Sales Email Prompt
Write a sales email for [PRODUCT]. Price: [PRICE] Benefits: [LIST 3 BENEFITS] Target: [AUDIENCE] Structure: 1. Hook (problem or curiosity) 2. Agitate the pain 3. Present solution 4. Benefits (not features) 5. Social proof 6. CTA with urgency 7. P.S. with bonus

4. Abandoned Cart Email

Cart Recovery Prompt
Write an abandoned cart recovery email sequence (3 emails). Product: [PRODUCT] Price: [PRICE] Email 1 (1 hour): Reminder + product image Email 2 (24 hours): Address objections + FAQ Email 3 (48 hours): Last chance + discount offer

Tips for Better Results

  • Always specify your target audience
  • Include examples of emails you like
  • Ask for multiple variations to choose from
  • Specify the tone (professional, casual, friendly)
  • Include constraints (word count, character limits)
