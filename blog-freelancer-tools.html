Freelancer Toolkit: 20 Free Tools to Grow Your Business

September 1, 2026 • 10 min read

Starting a freelance business doesn't require expensive tools. Here are 20 free tools to help you manage projects, create content, and grow your business.

Project Management

1. Notion

All-in-one workspace for notes, tasks, and databases. Free for personal use.

2. Trello

Kanban-style project management. Visual and easy to use.

3. Asana

Task management for teams. Free for up to 15 team members.

Design & Creative

4. Canva

Graphic design tool with thousands of templates. Free tier available.

5. Figma

UI/UX design tool. Free for up to 3 projects.

6. Loom

Screen recording for tutorials and demos. Free for 25 videos.

Writing & Content

7. Grammarly

Writing assistant for grammar and style. Free basic version.

8. Hemingway Editor

Make your writing clear and concise. Free to use.

9. ChatGPT

AI assistant for writing, research, and brainstorming. Free tier available.

Marketing & SEO

10. Google Analytics

Website analytics. Free for all websites.

11. Google Search Console

SEO monitoring and reporting. Free for all websites.

12. Ubersuggest

Keyword research tool. Free for 3 searches per day.

Communication

13. Slack

Team communication. Free for up to 10,000 messages.

14. Zoom

Video conferencing. Free for 40-minute meetings.

15. Calendly

Scheduling tool. Free for 1 calendar connection.

Finance & Invoicing

16. Wave

Free invoicing and accounting software.

17. PayPal

Payment processing. Free to receive payments.

18. Stripe

Online payment processing. Free to set up.

Productivity

19. Google Workspace

Docs, Sheets, Slides, and more. Free with Gmail account.

20. Todoist

Task management app. Free for up to 5 projects.
