How to Create a Landing Page That Converts

September 1, 2026 • 10 min read

A high-converting landing page turns visitors into customers. Here's the exact formula for creating pages that convert at 5% or higher.

The Landing Page Formula

5-Step Formula

1. Headline — Clear benefit in 10 words or less

2. Subheadline — Expand on the benefit

3. Social Proof — Testimonials, logos, numbers

4. Benefits — Not features, but outcomes

5. CTA — One clear action to take

1. Headline That Hooks

Your headline must grab attention in 3 seconds. Use these formulas:

  • "Get [BENEFIT] Without [PAIN]"
  • "How to [ACHIEVE GOAL] in [TIMEFRAME]"
  • "The [ADJECTIVE] Way to [GOAL]"

2. Subheadline That Expands

Support your headline with more detail. Explain who it's for and what they'll get.

3. Social Proof That Builds Trust

Show evidence that others have succeeded:

  • Customer testimonials (with photos)
  • Logos of companies you've worked with
  • Numbers ("2,500+ customers")
  • Ratings and reviews

4. Benefits That Sell

Focus on outcomes, not features:

  • Feature: "50+ templates"
  • Benefit: "Save 10 hours per week"
  • Feature: "SEO optimized"
  • Benefit: "Rank #1 on Google"

5. CTA That Converts

Make your CTA clear and compelling:

  • Use action words ("Get", "Start", "Join")
  • Remove friction ("Instant access", "No credit card")
  • Create urgency ("Limited time", "Only X left")
  • Make it stand out (contrasting color)
